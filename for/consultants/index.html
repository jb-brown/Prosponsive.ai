    Set up a workflow once — morning briefing, client digest, market monitoring, research synthesis — and Prosponsive runs it on schedule without you.     Your client documents, conversation history, credentials, and workflow data never leave your machine. Prosponsive runs entirely on your desktop — no cloud routing for your work. Your data stays with you, not with a provider. Encrypted locally. Offline-capable with local AI models.
