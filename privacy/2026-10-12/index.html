4. Text Messaging (SMS) Program

Prosponsive operates an SMS program in which users receive AI-assisted help messages from Prosponsive's AI Agent. Replies are generated by artificial intelligence. Users can opt in in any of these ways:

  • By pressing 9 during a call to a Prosponsive phone number, after hearing a consent disclosure.
  • By texting a Prosponsive phone number directly, in which case we will reply to your message. Our first reply includes program details and opt-out instructions.
  • By checking an unchecked consent box on a Prosponsive web form after seeing a consent disclosure.

What we collect: your mobile number, the content of messages you send and receive, and a record of your opt-in (phone number, timestamp, and the method you used to opt in, including a call recording if you opted in by phone).

How we use it: only to operate the SMS program, provide the AI Agent's responses, keep consent records, and honor opt-out requests. Message content may be processed by service providers that run the AI Agent and deliver messages on our behalf, under contracts that keep the data confidential and limit its use to providing the service. These providers are listed in Section 5.

Opt-out preferences: You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. After you opt out we will send you a confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe. We record and honor every opt-out request.

No sharing: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. All other sharing described in this policy excludes SMS opt-in data and consent.

Program details: Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase or of using our services. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. For support, email privacy@prosponsive.ai.
